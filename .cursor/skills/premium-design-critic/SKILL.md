# Premium Design Critic

Do not assume the work is good. Do not praise it without a critique. Identify weak areas and fix them before declaring the task complete.

## Review as

A CEO · a CMO · a senior designer · a conversion strategist · an SEO professional · a software engineer · a potential client

## Ask

- Does this look premium?
- Does it look templated?
- Does it look AI-generated?
- Is anything visually unnecessary?
- Is the hierarchy obvious?
- Is the copy saying something meaningful?
- Does every section earn its space?
- Are we showing evidence or making claims?
- Does the website make Apereel feel like a serious consultancy?
- Would I trust this company with an important digital project?

## How to report (then fix)

- **Must fix** — templated look, generic copy, fake proof, broken hierarchy, a11y/perf regressions
- **Should fix** — repeated sections, weak evidence, noisy motion, vague CTAs
- **Leave** — only if it already serves a senior reader

How to fix visuals: `premium-art-direction`. Copy: `conversion-copy`. Proof: `confidentiality-claims`. Do not restate those skills; judge the outcome.
