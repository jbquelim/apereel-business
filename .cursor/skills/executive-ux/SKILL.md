# Executive Website UX & Information Architecture

Design for a busy senior reader. Within about **10 seconds** the page must make clear:

1. What Apereel does
2. Who it helps
3. Why its philosophy is different
4. What business problems it solves
5. Why it is credible
6. What to do next

## Hierarchy

- One primary idea per section. Headline carries the point; body supports it.
- Important destinations should be reachable in about **three meaningful interactions** from the homepage. Apply the 3-click idea intelligently, not as a mechanical sitemap rule.
- Prioritize: clarity, scanning, navigation, service discovery, mobile, CTA visibility, logical progression.

## Do

- Lead with the decision, then evidence.
- Keep nav short: Approach, Services, Work, About, Contact (or equivalent). Do not add levels without a job.
- Make the next action obvious without repeating the same CTA in every block.
- CTA wording and density: `conversion-optimization`. Placement and scan path: this skill.

## Never

- Long landing pages with no hierarchy
- Walls of text
- Extra navigation tiers
- Repeated sections that restate the same idea
- Excessive card grids

CTA copy is not owned here — `conversion-copy` writes it, `conversion-optimization` chooses which CTA and how often.
