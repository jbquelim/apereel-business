# Premium Web Art Direction

The site must look like a premium management consultancy, enterprise technology firm, or sophisticated digital product studio — custom, not templated.

Would a CEO believe this company charges premium consulting rates? If not, change the design.

## Direction

Deep navy · white · rich blue · restrained red accents · premium typography · strong grids · large editorial type · generous whitespace · excellent photography · purposeful hierarchy · subtle motion · sophisticated data visualization

Every visual choice needs a reason.

## Do

- Let type, space, and a few precise accents do the work.
- Prefer editorial layouts over card-of-cards.
- Motion: short, functional (nav, focus, reveal). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` (`responsive-accessibility`).
- Charts and diagrams must explain a relationship, not decorate.
- Photography and generated visuals: `ai-creative-direction`.

## Never (AI-site clichés)

Excessive gradients · purple neon · wall-to-wall glassmorphism · floating blobs · random glowing orbs · every section in a rounded card · meaningless illustrations · stock handshake photos · excessive animation · generic SaaS dashboards-as-hero

## Combine

After visual work: `premium-design-critic`. Performance of assets: `web-performance`.
