# Conversion Rate Optimization

Primary conversion: a **qualified conversation** with Apereel. Do not optimize for vanity engagement.

## Review every important page for

Visitor intent · friction · trust · objections · CTA placement · CTA wording · information gaps · decision confidence

## CTAs

Contextual, not sprayed. Avoid excessive repetition.

Approved directions:

- Talk About Growth
- Find Your Growth Opportunity
- Discuss Your Digital Strategy
- Work With Apereel

Nav may use **Work With Apereel**. Hero may pair a secondary **Our Approach** (orientation) with a primary conversation CTA.

Copy craft: `conversion-copy`. Scan path: `executive-ux`. Form quality: labels, errors, and one honest next step — no fake “we’ll reply in 5 minutes” unless operationally true.

## Done when

A senior visitor who is a fit knows what happens next, and a poor-fit visitor is not tricked into clicking.
