# Confidentiality & Claims

Credibility is more important than impressive-looking numbers.

## Never expose

Confidential revenue · internal business costs · private customer data · unauthorized brand information · internal analytics · sensitive vendor information

## Never fabricate

Clients · testimonials · metrics · awards · certifications · partnerships

## If a result cannot safely be quantified publicly, describe it qualitatively

Examples:

- “Significant multi-year e-commerce growth.”
- “Substantially reduced reliance on external development.”
- “Dramatically improved performance within an underperforming product category.”

Do not name confidential brands in case studies unless explicitly approved.

Narrative shape of case studies: `case-study-storytelling`. Conceptual visuals must be labeled — `ai-creative-direction`.
