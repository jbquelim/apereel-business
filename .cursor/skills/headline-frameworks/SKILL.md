# Headline Frameworks

Write headlines that a CEO reads and thinks: "These people get it."

## Principles

- **One idea per headline.** If it needs a comma, it is two headlines.
- **Verb-forward.** Lead with action, not description.
- **Concrete over clever.** Specificity beats wordplay.
- **Tension creates attention.** Challenge an assumption, then resolve it.
- **Read it at 72px.** If it does not punch at billboard scale, rewrite.

## Proven B2B headline patterns

### 1. Command + Outcome
> Grow the business. The traffic follows.
> Build market share. Not just impressions.

### 2. Challenge + Resolution
> Most agencies optimize for clicks. We optimize for revenue.
> You don't need more traffic. You need more customers.

### 3. Reframe
> The best SEO strategy is a better business.
> Your strongest marketing asset is your product.

### 4. Confident Authority
> We grow businesses. Digitally.
> Digital growth. Business first.

### 5. Bold Claim
> The agency that starts with your P&L, not your keywords.
> Growth that starts before the first ad runs.

### 6. Contrast (Line 1 vs Line 2)
> Line 1: The expected thing (what others do, or the problem)
> Line 2: The Apereel flip (in accent color)

## Rules

- Maximum 8 words per line for hero headlines.
- Line 2 (accent color) should land the differentiator or payoff.
- Supporting text (1-2 sentences) earns the right to the bold claim.
- Never start with "We help" or "We are." Start with the outcome or the challenge.
- Test: cover the brand name. If the headline could belong to any agency, rewrite.
- Avoid questions in hero headlines. Statements create authority. Questions create doubt.

## Process

1. Start with 10+ raw headline candidates. Quantity before quality.
2. Kill anything generic (could apply to any agency).
3. Kill anything over 8 words per line.
4. Score survivors on: specificity, tension, memorability, brand-fit.
5. Present top 3-5 with the supporting text that earns each claim.

## Apereel-specific constraints

- The differentiator is **business first** — starting with the actual business strength, not tactics.
- The audience is **operators** (CEOs, CMOs, e-commerce directors) who run real businesses.
- The tone is **confident, direct, commercial** — not inspirational, not poetic.
- Never position Apereel as a generic digital/SEO agency.
- Refer to `brand-strategy` for positioning and `conversion-copy` for voice.
