# Case Study Storytelling

The reader should think: “This person saw the underlying business problem instead of just applying another marketing tactic.”

The most important part is the **reasoning**, not the tactic list.

## Structure (use this order)

1. **Business Problem** — what was commercially stuck
2. **Observation** — what was actually true in the business or market
3. **Insight** — the non-obvious cause
4. **Action** — what was changed
5. **Business Impact** — qualitative unless a number is approved
6. **Lesson** — what this implies for similar businesses

## Experience you may draw from (do not invent new categories of proof)

Inventory depth and organic visibility · faster inventory publishing · e-commerce UX improvements · product filtering · colour swatches · sticky filters · filter prioritization · 3-click product discovery · competitive pricing analysis · AI-assisted internal web development · reduced dependence on external development · luxury-brand digital compliance · launching new online brand-partner programs

## Never

- Fabricate statistics
- Expose confidential revenue
- Name confidential brands unless explicitly approved

Quantified claims: `confidentiality-claims`. Visuals: `premium-art-direction`. Search titles/headings: `business-first-seo`.
